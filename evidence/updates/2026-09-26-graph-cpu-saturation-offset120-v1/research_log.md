## 2026-09-26 — Graph CPU saturation with additive 120 W reporting offset

Ran a fresh fixed-16-ID SQLite graph HTTP sweep at client concurrency 1–128. All 13 levels returned exact frozen-oracle targets with zero failures. The highest concurrent throughput was 460.9 servings/s at concurrency 8; concurrency 8–128 stayed within 435.4–460.9/s while median CPU-package RAPL readings ranged 104.1–110.9 W. Preserved those raw readings and derived a separate `RAPL + 120 W` series; the offset is not measured wall power. Throughput at concurrency 1/4/8 was 2.5–6.1% lower than the prior clean sweep, while package power was 16.3–26.0 W higher. No CPU frequency, temperature, utilization or concurrent package-workload telemetry was captured, so the run-to-run difference remains unexplained. Source data, offset derivation, report and three graphs are indexed in `docs/benchmarks.md` and the experiment report. Since this updates the graph-serving baseline, archived exact `PAPER-v0001` and issued canonical paper `PAPER-v0002`, with the new limitation and evidence map.
