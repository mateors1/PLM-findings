# Findings catalog

[Back to the overview](../README.md) · [Evidence reader guide](../docs/reader-guide.md)

For a first pass, read **01 → 02 → 13**: question, results, then architecture and applications. The remaining papers examine particular methods, failures and evidence boundaries. Paper numbers identify topics, not a requirement to read everything in order.

1. [Research question and protocol](01-research-question-and-protocol.md) — what the experiment can establish.
2. [Learning and quality results](02-learning-and-quality.md) — the measured progression and failed interventions.
3. [Set prediction and failure analysis](03-set-prediction.md) — why complete answers remain difficult.
4. [Serving and numerical reproducibility](04-serving-and-reproducibility.md) — what application and performance tests established.
5. [Evidence ledger and next experiments](05-evidence-ledger.md) — claim status, source map, and acceptance work still open.
6. [Academic versioning and the next hypothesis](06-academic-versioning-and-next-hypothesis.md) — 23 final-checkpoint identities, remaining archival work, and learned relational retrieval.
7. [Rank ordering and threshold limits](07-ranking-and-threshold-limits.md) — why adding larger unions or tuning cutoffs alone cannot exceed the current selector's exact-set count on frozen evidence.
8. [A failed hardest-boundary margin](08-hardest-boundary-margin-failure.md) — a fresh paired training screen regressed from 192 to 6 exact answers, with both checkpoints and the failed gates preserved.
9. [Gradient strength and interference](09-gradient-strength-and-interference.md) — fixed training-query probes reveal substantial gradients and local opposition, with explicit limits on causal and optimizer claims.
10. [A weaker margin gives a mixed result](10-weaker-margin-mixed-result.md) — exact answers improve from 191 to 194, but separation falls from 168 to 164; the unchanged gate rejects the variant.
11. [Candidate availability and selection](11-candidate-availability-and-selection.md) — both saved selectors find every available exact answer; missing candidates limit the remaining exactness.
12. [FP8 inference screen](12-fp8-inference-screen.md) — eager FP8 kept similar validation quality but was much slower on the measured native path.
13. [Decoder-only architecture and applications](13-decoder-and-applications.md) — why this reference model generates IDs sequentially, and which uses remain hypotheses.
